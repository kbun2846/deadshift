deadshift alpha v0.9 is a minimalist top down shooter with distinct weapons and explosive combat, playable in the browser on mobile or pc

- three distinct weapons: static, nominal and ballast
- online multiplayer for up to 4 players: host a game, share the room code, and play rounds from a lobby
  - free-for-all with a round clock and kill limit, or practice with targets where nothing counts
  - host settings: spawns, round length, kill limit, health, respawn wait
  - weapon pick at the start of each round, pings, kill feed and scoreboard
- weapon tutorials, solo practice and 1v1 against a robot
- built for multiple maps, with more to come; the alpha is tested on deadwater outpost, a desert town with enterable buildings, destructible props and a crop field that burns
- touch controls with an editable layout, five graphics presets

## what's new in v0.9

- 1v1 against a robot: pick the map, your weapon, its weapon (or random), skill from rookie to expert, its aim and temper, first to 3, 5, 10 or endless
- robots blend play styles and shift between calm and aggressive during a fight
- new x abilities: nova (nominal) and blast (ballast); static's hex hits harder
- ballast reworked: one press, one shell; no more charging
- menus show weapon and map pictures, with coming-soon slots
- target lock by turning to face someone or with the arrow keys; no auto-lock
- fairer camera on every screen, see-through touch buttons with rounded corners
- hear gunfire by direction (pink ring), kill and one-shot popups, syphon healing
- multiplayer freeze fixed, lots of bug fixes and smoother loading

## what's new in v0.82

- enemies or allies; allies stay by you and help you
- eight robot skins, never two alike in a game
- each robot has its own skill and play style
- robot previews in the multiplayer menus and a 1v1 page (not working yet)
